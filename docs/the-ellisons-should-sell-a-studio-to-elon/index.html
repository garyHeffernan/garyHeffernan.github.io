The Ellisons Should Sell a Studio to Elon

× Date: Wed, Aug 12, 2026

Argument: the Ellison family should divest one of the film studios (Paramount Pictures or Warner Bros.) to Elon Musk. In three points.

  1. Rob Bonta will not budge on “behavioral” remedies (e.g. agreements to release a certain number of films annually). He won’t move off of his position that he wants “structural” remedies (a breakup of the company) as part of any settlement.

Rob Bonta has been steadfast in his commitment that the Paramount - Warner Bros. Discovery merger is unlawful and would require structural remedies to close. He has repeated this point even after it became common knowledge that Ellison would make a commitment to release a set number of films in theaters as a behavioral remedy. If Bonta won’t accept a behavioral remedy on the film side, then the easiest path to close the deal would be a divestiture of one studio by Ellison. His appetite to do so is unclear, but there is one way it could work.

Assume Ellison has to divest a film studio to close the deal. What’s the least painful way to accomplish this? Divest to an ally.

Divesting to an ally retains business relationships between the two studios. For the sake of argument, let’s assume Ellison divests Warner Bros. Studios to Elon Musk. What does this accomplish?

  • Warner Bros. films still appear on Ellison’s streaming service, Paramount+, even though he doesn’t control the production of those films. This is easily achieved through a contractual agreement, and is something that could be worked out with an ally.
  • There is no antitrust case in selling to Elon Musk. He is an individual. He could also acquire it via SpaceX or another one of his companies. The point still stands: there is no antitrust risk in Elon Musk or a Musk-controlled company acquiring a film studio.
  • Importantly, a divestiture to Musk creates a new competitor in film that could stand on its own. Antitrust regulators care about competition, and divesting an asset just to get under a market concentration percentage is of no use if the divested assets wither and die in their new form. The divested studio will have the resources of the world’s richest man. And being one of the biggest studios in the world, it would have no problem standing on its own, freed from the Discovery Global cable networks.
  • Musk’s control of the film studio creates an opportunity for him to invent a new studio business model, one heavily reliant on AI and data centers. He could use the studio to reimagine what it means to make movies, consistent with his X.com post that he will release an AI-generated version of The Odyssey this year (see next point).
  1. Musk has a fixation with Hollywood

Hollywood is on Elon Musk’s mind, and it has been for years. He has tweeted things like “Cancel Netflix for the health of your kids”, “Cancel Netflix”, “Popcorn Emoji” in a response to Paramount’s hostile bid for Warner Bros. in Dec 2025, “Disney has a major content problem. Almost their entire upcoming slate is unwatchable. They are the world’s biggest example of go woke, go broke lol.” , and most recently “Before this year ends, Grok Imagine will make a full-length movie of The Odyssey that is historically accurate and true to the art of Homer”. Musk felt the need to buy Twitter to fix social media to his liking. He could very well do the same with Hollywood.

  1. Larry Ellison and Elon Musk are close

Musk and Ellison are close. Ellison remarked on his friendship with Musk in 2018 when he revealed a stake in Tesla: “My second-largest investment, I will disclose it now, I am not sure people know I am very close friends to Elon Musk and I am a very big investor in Tesla,” Ellison said on a call. Anyone who followed Musk’s takeover of Twitter is familiar with the text exchange between Ellison and Musk, asking Ellison to finance the deal for a billion or two dollars. Finally, it was revealed in a recent NYT article that Ellison is building a house on the island of Lanai for his good friend Musk, whom the Times described as “a regular visitor”. These two are close which makes it easier to get a deal done at all, not to mention one done quickly and at the right price.
